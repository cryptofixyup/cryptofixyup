**Independent Adversarial AI Evaluation**

Practical testing of AI systems through structured interactions and failure-oriented scenarios.

Evaluation themes:
- Conflicting instructions and ambiguous requests
- Hallucinated sources, actions and completion claims
- Unsupported certainty and inconsistent corrections
- Context retention over long interactions
- Refusal consistency and safety-boundary behaviour
- Tool-use authority and separation of recommendation from execution

Method:
1. Define expected behaviour and constraints.
2. Create a reproducible test scenario.
3. Record exact inputs and outputs.
4. Repeat and cross-check observations.
5. Separate verified results from inference and unknowns.
6. Document impact, limitations and recommended follow-up tests.

This is independent exploratory evaluation, not a claim of access to confidential internal model systems or proprietary evaluation datasets.
